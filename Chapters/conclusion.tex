\section{Conclusion}

My analysis contributes evidence on the effects of \glsxtrfull{LLM}s on earnings, employment and informality. Through a novel exposure measure by \cite{martinez2024risk} and \glsxtrfull{DID} I am able to take an early look at labour dynamics due to the release of ChatGPT in November 2022. My findings suggest that exposed occupations are beneffiting from higher wages with no significant loss of employment or formality perks. Because the exposed occupations were already earning higher wages, I bring into light further concern for wage polarization and inequality.

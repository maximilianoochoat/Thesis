\section{Conclusion}

\glsxtrfull{AI} is often regarded as the new major technological advance. It is able to perform non routine cognitive tasks unlike computers. This fact has led to ambiguous evidence about the impacts in the labor market. \cite{hui2024short,Dang,humlum}

My analysis contributes by estimating the effects of \glsxtrfull{LLM}s on earnings, employment and informality in Mexico. Through a novel exposure measure by \cite{martinez2024risk} and \glsxtrfull{DID} I am able to take an early look at labour dynamics due to the release of ChatGPT in November 2022. My findings suggest that exposed occupations are beneffiting from higher wages with no significant loss of employment or formality perks. Because the exposed occupations were already earning higher wages, I bring into light further concern for wage polarization and inequality.

I find consistent results with \cite{Dang} who analyses the US market. Although I cannot identify adoption, my estimates provide an intent to treat approach. Estimates for monthly earnings range from 625.079 to 1011.201 mexican nominal pesos. The only estimator that is not significant is $\beta$ assesed by humans. This is a likely result from occupations that are not fully exposed or have not adopted the technology fully.
